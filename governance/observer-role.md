# TC observer role

This document defines how a person who is not a TC Representative can follow the work of the Technical Committee before TC meetings open to the public.

## Who can be an observer

Any active participant in an AAIF Working Group or Technical Project can ask to observe. A request goes to the TC Chair or Co-Chair, by email to support@aaif.io or by a message in the AAIF Discord. The Chair or Co-Chair decides each request. They can decline a request or end an observer seat at any time, and they don't have to give a reason.

## What an observer can do

- Read the `#technical-committee` channel and the TC agendas and minutes.
- Attend TC meetings when the Chair invites observers to that meeting.
- Speak during a meeting when the Chair asks for observer input.

## What an observer can't do

- Vote, count toward quorum, or object under lazy consensus. Section 3 of the [TC Charter](charter.md) gives these rights to TC voting members only.
- Share material that the TC marks as confidential. Everything else follows the [Linux Foundation antitrust policy](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/antitrust-policy) that applies to every TC meeting.

## Record of observers

The TC lists current observers in the `Observers` section of the repository `README.md`, with the date each seat started.

## When this role ends

This role exists until TC meetings open to the public. After that date, anyone can attend, and the TC can retire this document.
