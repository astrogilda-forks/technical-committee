# TC agendas and minutes

The TC posts the agenda and the minutes for each meeting in this directory.

## File names

Each meeting has one file, named by its date in `YYYY-MM-DD.md` format, for example `2026-10-13.md`. The file holds the agenda before the meeting and the minutes after it.

## Timing

- The agenda is posted at least two business days before the meeting.
- The minutes are posted within five business days after the meeting.

## What the minutes record

- The TC Representatives and observers who attended, and whether quorum was met.
- Each decision, with the vote count or a note that it passed by lazy consensus.
- Action items, each with an owner.

Material that the TC marks as confidential is left out of the posted minutes, and the minutes say that something was left out.

## Standing agenda items

Every agenda carries these items:

1. Project proposals and lifecycle changes.
2. Working Group reports.
3. Conformance and testing input. Section 3(e)(viii) of the foundation charter asks the Governing Board to collect testing tools as input to conformance programs. This item gives Working Groups and Technical Projects a place to bring a test suite or a conformance result to the TC.
